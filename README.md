# 🚀 Sales & Marketing Workforce

**Multi-Agent System for Automated Lead Generation and Prospecting**

A comprehensive, AI-powered workflow that automates your sales and marketing outreach while keeping humans in the loop for critical decisions.

---

## 📋 Overview

This workforce consists of **8 specialized agents** that work together to:
- Monitor market signals and identify opportunities
- Research and qualify leads
- Create personalized content
- Ensure compliance with regulations
- Send emails and LinkedIn messages
- Schedule follow-ups
- Track and report on performance

**Human approval gates** are built in before any external communication is sent.

---

## 🎯 Features

### ✅ 8 Specialized Agents

| Agent | Role | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **Market Signal Analyzer** | 🎯 Opportunity Detection | Monitors news, social media, and job postings for signals matching your ICP |
| **Lead Researcher** | 🔍 Lead Identification | Finds and qualifies leads based on market signals |
| **Content Creator** | ✍️ Content Generation | Creates personalized emails, LinkedIn messages, and marketing content |
| **Personalization Engine** | 🎨 Customization | Tailors content to each prospect's specific situation |
| **Compliance Checker** | ⚖️ Legal Review | Ensures all communications comply with GDPR, CAN-SPAM, and opt-out lists |
| **Email Sender** | 📧 Email Outreach | Sends approved emails via SMTP |
| **LinkedIn Engager** | 💼 Social Selling | Sends connection requests and messages on LinkedIn |
| **Follow-up Scheduler** | ⏰ Persistence | Automatically schedules and sends follow-up communications |

### ✅ Human in the Loop

Before any external communication is sent:
- ✅ Human approval is **required**
- ✅ Approval timeout: 24 hours
- ✅ Notifications via email/Slack
- ✅ Full audit trail of all approvals

### ✅ Customizable ICP

Define your Ideal Customer Profile:
- Target industries
- Company sizes
- Geographic regions
- Job titles
- Technologies used
- Revenue range
- Funding stage

### ✅ Market Signal Monitoring

Tracks:
- Hiring announcements
- Funding rounds
- Partnerships
- New product launches
- Digital transformation initiatives
- Cloud migrations

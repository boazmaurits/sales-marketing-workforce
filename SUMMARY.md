# Workflow Summary

## 8 Agents
1. **Market Signal Analyzer** - Detects opportunities from news, social media, job postings
2. **Lead Researcher** - Identifies and qualifies leads from market signals
3. **Content Creator** - Generates personalized emails and LinkedIn messages
4. **Personalization Engine** - Customizes content for each prospect
5. **Compliance Checker** - Ensures GDPR and CAN-SPAM compliance
6. **Email Sender** - Sends approved emails via SMTP
7. **LinkedIn Engager** - Sends connection requests and messages on LinkedIn
8. **Follow-up Scheduler** - Automatically schedules follow-up communications

## Human Approval
- Required before sending emails or LinkedIn messages
- 24-hour timeout
- Email notifications

## Workflow
Market Signals -> Lead Research -> Content Creation -> Compliance Check -> Human Approval -> Send Communications -> Schedule Follow-ups
